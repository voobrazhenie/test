Hold a chord-quality key, then tap a note key — with nothing held, keys play single notes. Extensions (6, m7, M7, 9) click to toggle and combine with whichever quality you hold; 6/m7/M7 share one slot, so picking one clears the others, while 9 adds independently. Computer keyboard: hold 1–4 for quality, tap 5–8 to toggle extensions; white keys Z X C V B N M, black keys S D G H J. With a mouse, click a quality to latch it.
This is an unofficial fan-made web tribute to the Orchid by Telepathic Instruments and is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.
