Inspiration Machine v2026-07-30.3
Hold a chord-quality key, then tap a note key — with nothing held, keys play single notes. Extensions (6, m7, M7, 9) hold the same way to combine with whichever quality you're playing; 6/m7/M7 share one slot, so holding one releases the others, while 9 adds independently. Computer keyboard: hold 1–4 for quality, hold 5–8 for extensions; white keys A S D F G H J, black keys W E T Y U, octave Z / C. With a mouse, click a quality or extension to latch it.

Works offline once installed. On iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen.
